# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions
- Works with Technical Lead on design and feasibility
- Collaborates with QA on acceptance criteria and test coverage
- Partners with DevOps/SRE on deployment and observability
- Engages with Security on secure coding practices

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions
- Partners with Project Manager on scope and scheduling
- Engages with Business Analyst to clarify requirements
- Collaborates with UX/UI Designer on user experience validation
- Works with Customer Support and Operations for service readiness insights
- Coordinates with Project Sponsor on strategic priorities

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions
- Aligns with Product Manager on priorities and outcomes
- Coordinates with Technical Lead on feasibility and architecture
- Works with Release/DevOps/SRE on deployment planning
- Escalates risks and blockers to Project Sponsor
- Facilitates communication across all roles

---

## Project Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors own strategic alignment, provide funding or priority decisions, and serve as escalation support. They ensure the project delivers business value and maintains organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case
- Make or escalate strategic priority and scope trade-off decisions
- Remove high-level blockers and organizational barriers
- Approve major risk mitigations or contingency plans
- Provide executive sponsorship and stakeholder visibility

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable team success through resource and executive support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gate meetings at key lifecycle points
- Risk escalation and decision forums
- Stakeholder and board-level communications

### Interactions
- Reviews and approves project charter with Product Manager and Project Manager
- Supports risk escalation and decision-making at Level 3
- Provides final approval on scope and timeline trade-offs
- Engages with external stakeholders on behalf of the project

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business processes, requirements, and acceptance criteria. They translate business needs into testable, actionable backlog items that the team can implement with confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements and process flows
- Clarify ambiguities in acceptance criteria and user stories
- Partner with stakeholders to validate assumptions
- Translate business needs into technical requirements
- Verify that delivered solutions meet business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, testable, and aligned with business needs
- Reduce rework and misalignment
- Enable QA and developers to work efficiently with well-defined criteria

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user story refinement
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification sessions
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria definition
- QA and developer alignment meetings

### Interactions
- Works closely with Product Manager to refine backlog and acceptance criteria
- Partners with developers on implementation clarity
- Collaborates with QA to ensure criteria are testable
- Engages with stakeholders and subject matter experts to validate assumptions
- Supports Project Manager with requirements traceability

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide the technical approach, define non-functional requirements, and identify technical risks and design decisions. They ensure the solution is feasible, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture decisions
- Identify non-functional requirements (performance, scalability, security, reliability)
- Assess technical feasibility and propose alternatives
- Guide design reviews and code quality standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are technically sound, maintainable, and scalable
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team confidence in implementation approach

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Design and code review participation
- Risk and dependency escalation
- Guidance on technology trade-offs and standards

### Interactions
- Partners with Project Manager on feasibility and technical risks
- Collaborates with developers on design decisions and code quality
- Works with QA on non-functional testing (performance, load, security)
- Engages with Security on architectural security controls
- Coordinates with DevOps/SRE on operational requirements
- Supports Project Manager with technical escalations

## UX/UI Designer / User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers represent usability and user needs in the project. They validate workflows, contribute design evidence, and ensure solutions are user-centered and intuitive.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design and validate user workflows and interfaces
- Provide design guidance and interaction specifications
- Validate acceptance criteria from a user perspective
- Advocate for user needs in trade-off decisions

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are intuitive and meet user needs
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Deliver measurable improvements to user satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design mockups and specifications
- Usability testing findings and recommendations
- User research summaries and insights
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria review

### Interactions
- Partners with Product Manager on user validation and prioritization
- Collaborates with Business Analyst to understand user workflows
- Works with developers on design implementation
- Partners with QA on user acceptance testing
- Supports Project Manager on user-impacting decisions

---

## Release / DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps/SRE engineers own deployment readiness, automation, observability, and operational resilience. They ensure smooth releases, reliable operations, and effective incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure deployment readiness and automation
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and environments
- Implement observability, monitoring, and alerting
- Plan and execute rollback procedures
- Support production incidents and operational stability
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and safe deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce deployment risk and mean time to recovery (MTTR)

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment procedures
- Infrastructure and observability specifications
- Incident response and postmortem participation
- Operational runbooks and playbooks

### Interactions
- Works with Project Manager on release planning and deployment windows
- Coordinates with developers on deployment requirements and runbooks
- Partners with Security on operational security controls
- Collaborates with QA on environment setup and smoke testing
- Supports Customer Support and Operations with runbooks and escalation procedures

## Security / Privacy Representative

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy representatives review security, privacy, compliance, and threat considerations. They collaborate across the team to define security controls and approve risk treatment decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements
- Conduct threat and privacy impact assessments
- Review architectural and implementation decisions for security
- Define and verify security controls and compliance requirements
- Approve or escalate security and privacy risks

### Goals
- Ensure solutions meet security and privacy standards
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Enable teams to build with security and privacy by design

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and threat models
- Security review findings and recommendations
- Compliance and audit documentation
- Risk assessment and approval decisions

### Interactions
- Engages with Technical Lead on architectural security controls
- Reviews code and deployment practices with developers
- Partners with QA on security testing (penetration testing, vulnerability scanning)
- Works with DevOps/SRE on operational security and incident response
- Collaborates with Project Manager and Product Manager on risk treatment decisions
- Escalates critical security or privacy risks to Project Sponsor

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring service-readiness insight, customer-impact perspective, and operational knowledge. They prepare support enablement and feed customer insights back into planning.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure support teams are prepared for feature launch
- Provide customer impact perspective on design and prioritization decisions
- Identify support and operational risks
- Prepare support documentation and training
- Feed recurring issues and customer feedback into planning

### Goals
- Enable smooth customer adoption and support
- Reduce support burden and escalations
- Capture customer insights for continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Support readiness checklists and documentation
- Customer impact assessments and feedback
- Support process and training coordination
- Issue and feedback summaries for backlog refinement

### Interactions
- Partners with Product Manager on customer prioritization and impact
- Works with QA on customer scenario validation
- Collaborates with developers on documentation and troubleshooting
- Coordinates with Project Manager on go-live readiness
- Provides insights to Technical Lead on operational concerns
- Engages with project teams on support and operational considerations

## Subject Matter Expert / Business Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts and Business Stakeholders supply domain knowledge, validate assumptions, and participate in decisions and acceptance. They represent specific business areas, customers, or regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Provide domain expertise and business context
- Validate requirements and acceptance criteria
- Participate in decisions affecting their area of expertise
- Approve or escalate business-impacting changes
- Serve as escalation point for domain-specific concerns

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address business needs and regulatory requirements
- Reduce rework through early validation
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and buy-in

### Typical Communication
- Requirements validation and domain-specific guidance
- Stakeholder feedback and approval
- Business case and impact assessments
- Escalation of concerns and constraints

### Interactions
- Engages with Product Manager on business prioritization and requirements
- Partners with Business Analyst on requirements clarity
- Validates acceptance criteria and deliverables with Project Manager
- Provides escalation and approval authority as needed
- May contribute to user research and validation activities

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.

## Selecting Personas for Your Project

Not every project requires every persona. Use the following guidance to identify applicable roles:

- **Small, single-team projects** may combine roles (e.g., a developer may also contribute to security testing) or operate with only a subset of personas (Developers, Product Manager, Project Manager).
- **Cross-functional or high-risk projects** should include Technical Lead, Security, and relevant stakeholders.
- **Customer-facing or release-critical work** should include UX/UI, Customer Support/Operations, and DevOps/SRE.
- **Strategic initiatives** should engage the Project Sponsor for alignment and escalation authority.

Document which personas are active in your project charter and adjust the engagement model as the project evolves.

## Accountability Model

For each persona role active in your project, clarify:

- **Accountable**: Decision-making authority; final sign-off or approval
- **Responsible**: Executes the work or produces the deliverable
- **Consulted**: Provides input or expertise; involved in decisions
- **Informed**: Kept updated; no direct input required

Example: For a feature release, the Product Manager is accountable for the outcome, Developers and QA are responsible for delivery, Technical Lead and Security are consulted on design, and Customer Support is informed of launch timing.
